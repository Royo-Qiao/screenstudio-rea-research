# Community conduct

Keep discussions respectful, specific and useful. Critique technical claims and
methods, not people. Harassment, threats, discrimination and disclosure of another
person's private information are not acceptable.

Do not attach private recordings, captured keystrokes, license material or vendor
binaries. Use a minimal synthetic example or aggregate report when reporting a
problem. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for sensitive reports.

Maintainers may edit or remove violating content and restrict participation to
protect contributors. These expectations apply to maintainers and contributors
in this repository's issues, pull requests and other project-managed spaces.
